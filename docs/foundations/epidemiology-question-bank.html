Epidemiology (CM7) — Question Bank

Epidemiology (CM7): Question Bank

A written companion to the four decks that carry the 15 CM7 lectures: Chapter 3: Principles of Epidemiology (Lectures 1–5), Chapter 4: Epidemiologic Methods (Lectures 6–9), Chapter 6: Communicable Diseases (Lectures 10–13), and Chapter 5: Screening (Lectures 14–15).

Questions are organized by lecture. Descriptive questions (long essay, short essay, short notes, reasoning, case scenario) are for written practice. MCQs (single best answer, multiple correct, assertion–reason) are followed by a lettered answer key. Try them before checking. The same 270 MCQs appear as interactive Self-Check slides at the end of each lecture in the decks.

Sources: the faculty question bank (Section V, Chapter 19 Principles and Practice of Epidemiology), review exercises from Community Medicine with Recent Advances (9th ed.), Section 3, Chapters 9–21 and its University Examination Questions (listed under each lecture), the decks’ earlier Self-Check questions, and new questions written for the thinner lectures (10–15).

Assertion–reason options (all A-R items): a. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A · b. Both A and R are true, but R is NOT the correct explanation of A · c. A is true, R is false · d. A is false, R is true.

Notes on the source keys:

  • Ch 19 MCQ 27 (temporality): as printed, both A and R are false and there is no “both false” option. The assertion has been reworded to “Temporality is an essential criterion for concluding causation” so that the printed key (c) is correct.
  • Latest NFHS: the textbook answer was NFHS-5. NFHS-6 (2023–24) fact sheets were released in May 2026, so the option set and key are updated (retrieved 28 Oct 2026).
  • Sources of Information (Ch 11) MCQs: the answer key was not in the images supplied. The answers here follow Park / RGI / MoHFW sources.
  • Case-control helmet exercise (Ch 18, Q13): the printed answer (OR 1.22, etiological fraction 18%) is an arithmetic error. (55 × 68) ÷ (45 × 32) = 2.6, and EF = (OR − 1)/OR ≈ 61%. The key here uses the corrected values.
  • Cohort Q2 (Ch 17): the printed key, “preventive factors cannot be studied by a cohort study”, is kept. Observational cohorts can still show protective associations.
  • Screening Q8 (Ch 19): the stem prints “very high specificity and only moderate specificity”. The second word should be “sensitivity”.
  • Digital health (Ch 21): NDHM (2020) is now the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM, 2021). The explanations note this.
  • First step in epidemic investigation (Ch 20, Q1): the textbook and Park put verification of the diagnosis first. The CDC 10-step list puts establishing the outbreak first. The decks show both.
